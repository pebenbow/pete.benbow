An introduction to database programming. Using SQL, students learn to explore and interact with databases, create and manipulate data, extract knowledge through analytics and visualization, and apply fundamental database design principles. While the course focuses predominantly on the applied use of relational databases, it also introduces neighboring topics like non-relational data, APIs, extract-transform-load (ETL) processes, and business intelligence (BI) systems.
