### Custom PC (2025)

<img src="/images/projects/custom-pc2025.jpg" alt="Custom-built PC, 2025" style="max-height:500px" />

My latest creation does away with all the RGB lighting that my previous build had, instead going for a more discreet aesthetic. To get there, I bought a Fractal Design North case, plus a brand new motherboard, CPU, PSU, 64GB RAM, and an AIO cooler. These new parts were all specifically curated to avoid any RGB lighting, with the exception of the temperature display on the AIO cooler's CPU mount. Carried over from my previous build was the RTX 3070 GPU and my storage config.

This thing runs like a dream and I love the way it looks, especially how the walnut accents on the front of the case coordinate with my Ikea desk top and my Edifier speakers.

### Custom PC (2020)

<img src="/images/projects/custom-pc2020.jpg" alt="Custom-built PC, 2020" style="max-height:500px" />

This was the first custom PC I ever built myself. I ordered the parts for this
in March 2020, literally one week before the whole world went on lockdown due
to COVID-19. If I had waited any longer, I might have never gotten these
components because suddenly everybody decided to use the pandemic as an excuse to
get into gaming, plus the crypto-bros started to buy up all the GPUs, leading
to worldwide scarcity and price-gouging. Those were weird days...

I did a fair amount of gaming on this, but I also used it as my video editing/production
station when I was recording all the content for my Power BI and SQL courses on edX.
It served me really well until 2025 when I replaced it with my newest rig.
